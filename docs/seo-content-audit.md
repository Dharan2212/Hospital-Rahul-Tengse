### Per-Blog SEO Requirements
Each blog article needs:
- Unique `<title>` in format: `{Article Title} | Dr. Rahul Bhaskarrao Tengse`
- Meta description 130–155 chars
- `og:type = "article"`
- `Article` schema (author, datePublished, publisher, headline, description)
- One H1 matching the article title
- H2/H3 hierarchy within article
- Canonical URL
- Featured image with alt text
- Medical disclaimer at bottom of every article
